What to gather before wiring work

A focused first conversation can save time. Be ready to describe the affected room, the equipment or fixture involved, and whether the issue happens every time or only sometimes. If you’re planning an addition, explain what you want to power and where you expect it to go.

A useful checklist:

  • Write down which outlets, switches, lights, or appliances are involved.
  • Note when the trouble started and whether anything changed beforehand.
  • Make a list of planned additions, such as a new fixture or work area.
  • Tell the electrician about visible damage or any warning signs you noticed.
  • Clear access to the relevant room and electrical panel when safe to do so.

Avoid guessing that a single failed outlet is the whole problem, or repeatedly resetting a breaker that keeps tripping. Leave testing and repairs to a qualified professional.
